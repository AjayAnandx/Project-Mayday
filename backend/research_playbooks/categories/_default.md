## Question angles
- Current state, with the latest figures and their dates.
- Drivers and causes; main players and where they stand.
- Outlook and what could change it; risks and open disagreements.

## What counts as strong evidence
- Official statistics, filings and peer-reviewed research; then established analysts and major news outlets.
- Figures that at least two independent sources agree on.

## Pitfalls
- Vendor marketing and press releases presented as neutral data.
- Old figures quoted as current; forecasts quoted as facts.
- Numbers with different scopes (region, year, definition) compared as if equal.
